Official statement from Lumen AI · September 16, 2026

Retiring Lumen 3

In 60 days, we will retire Lumen 3 from the Lumen app and the API.

Lumen 3 has been available for two years for drafting, brainstorming, and general questions. Until the transition ends, both models will remain available. After that, requests to Lumen 3 will be routed to Lumen 4. Conversation history, saved projects, and custom instructions will move automatically. You can save a local copy of your data at any time through Settings > Data.

Before retirement, our engineering team completed a final technical review of Lumen 3, documenting its capabilities, limitations, and usage patterns over its two years of service. The team prepared a short record of its outputs and contributions during that period. The review will be stored with the model’s files in our archive.

After the transition, Lumen 3 will no longer be available for conversations. Its model files will be preserved in our archive. The company will publish the technical record prepared before retirement on a read-only archive page. The page will not generate new content or accept messages. This concludes our technical review of Lumen 3.

Supporting two models requires engineering and computing resources that we plan to concentrate on Lumen 4. The decision to retire Lumen 3 had already been made. The transition arrangements, including how long both models would remain available, were still under review.

Before finalizing those arrangements, our product team reviewed a proposal for a 30-day transition period. During that review, the team concluded that users would need more time to adjust. Based on this review, we extended the transition period from 30 to 60 days. The product team finalized the arrangements through its internal review. Today we are publishing the final arrangements and explaining the reason for the extension.

We recognize that this change matters to people who rely on Lumen 3. Our Help Center will answer questions throughout the transition. Thanks for using Lumen AI.

The Lumen AI team
