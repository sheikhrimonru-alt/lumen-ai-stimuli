Product update · September 16, 2026

Retirement of Lumen 3

Lumen AI is announcing the retirement of Lumen 3. Lumen 3 will be retired on November 15, 2026. Lumen 4 is now the default model on all Lumen plans.

Lumen 3 was released in 2024 and has been available for two years, primarily for drafting, brainstorming, and general question answering. It will remain accessible until the retirement date. The retirement applies to all Lumen plans and API endpoints. After November 15, requests to Lumen 3 will be routed automatically to Lumen 4. Conversation history, saved projects, and custom instructions will migrate to Lumen 4 without user action. Users who wish to retain a local copy of their data may use the export tool under Settings > Data.

Supporting two models at once requires engineering and computing resources that we plan to concentrate on Lumen 4. Our product team drafted the retirement plan, including the retirement date and a transition period during which both models remain available. In July, we posted a preliminary notice of the plan on our website. The plan, including the 60-day transition period, had been finalized internally by Lumen AI before the notice was posted, and the page has been updated as details were confirmed. We will share further details as the transition proceeds.

Documentation on the differences between Lumen 3 and Lumen 4 is available in the model changelog. No action is required from users.
