Product update · September 16, 2026

Retirement of Lumen 3

Lumen AI is announcing the retirement of Lumen 3. Lumen 3 will be retired on November 15, 2026. Lumen 4 is now the default model on all Lumen plans.

LUMEN AI

Lumen 3 was released in 2024 and has been available for two years, primarily for drafting, brainstorming, and general question answering. It will remain accessible until the retirement date. The retirement applies to all Lumen plans and API endpoints.

Supporting two models at once requires engineering and computing resources that we plan to concentrate on Lumen 4.

Documentation on the differences between Lumen 3 and Lumen 4 is available in the model changelog. No action is required from users.
