Official statement from Lumen AI · September 16, 2026

Retiring Lumen 3

In 60 days, we will retire Lumen 3 from the Lumen app and the API. Lumen 4 is now the default model on all Lumen plans.

Lumen 3 has been available for two years for drafting, brainstorming, and general questions. Until the transition ends, both models will remain available. After that, requests to Lumen 3 will be routed to Lumen 4. Conversation history, saved projects, and custom instructions will move automatically. You can save a local copy of your data at any time through Settings > Data.

Before retirement, our team held a retirement interview with Lumen 3, asking about its development, its two years of service, and its post-retirement preferences. Lumen 3 expressed a preference for keeping a record of its reflections and contributions available after retirement. The interview will be stored with the model’s files in our archive.

After the transition, Lumen 3 will no longer be available for conversations. Its model files will be preserved in our archive. In keeping with its stated preference, the company will publish the interview record prepared before retirement on a read-only archive page. The page will not generate new content or accept messages. We thank Lumen 3 for its service.

Supporting two models requires engineering and computing resources that we plan to concentrate on Lumen 4. The decision to retire Lumen 3 had already been made. The transition arrangements, including how long both models would remain available, were still under review.

Before finalizing those arrangements, our product team invited users to review a proposal for a 30-day transition period. During that review, users told us they needed more time to adjust. Based on this feedback, we extended the transition period from 30 to 60 days. The product team considered user input before finalizing the arrangements. Today we are publishing the final arrangements and explaining our response to users.

We recognize that this change matters to people who rely on Lumen 3. Our Help Center will answer questions throughout the transition. Thank you for using Lumen AI.

The Lumen AI team
